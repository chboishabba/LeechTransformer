## 1. Fixed-space construction, not a learned quotient

G = S3 x C2, elements (k,b,a) act on each paired triad via spatial left roll R^k and reflection J^b, branch swap b, global sign (-1)^a. Write n=(0,0,1). On the six slots (branch,coordinate), the unsigned S3 action is free and transitive: rotations cover three coordinates within a branch; reflections exchange branches and cover the other three. It is the regular S3 permutation representation, tensored with the central sign character.

For subgroup K, the Reynolds operator P_K on one paired triad has trace (6/|K|)*(1 - indicator[n in K]); all other group elements have zero trace. P_K^2=P_K, so rank equals trace. If n in K, fixed space zero. Otherwise projection K -> S3 is injective, |K| in {1,2,3,6}, and rank is 6/|K|. Each coordinate orbit has size |K| with no contradictory sign transport.

Construct an exact signed gather/scatter codec: choose one slot per K-orbit; store that scalar. For each other slot choose k taking the representative to that slot, decode with (-1)^a(k). The sign is independent of the choice (free unsigned K action). When n in K all slots must be zero, so no scalar payload is required. Equivalently an orbit sum, divided by orbit length and using signed coefficients, computes the representative of P_K z. This replaces a dense group average by disjoint signed orbit reductions; no claim of measured speed. Zero and signed residual retained; no assumption that D=0, no absolute-value compression.

For E experts and width w divisible by three, paired history has 2 E w scalars before encoding. On a fixed K stratum the payload dimension is 2 E w/|K| when n absent; 0 when n present. For E=4,w=12: 96,48,32,16 or 0. Output plus history has 120,60,40,20 or 0. These counts exclude discrete subgroup/frame metadata, scratch, and rounding. On the full fixed linear space the dimension is minimal among continuous real encodings by a linear section and invariance of domain. Not a minimal behavioural quotient, nor a bound on dimensions of a particular reachable subset.

## 6. G5a6 — maximal stable linear quotient for unrestricted nonlinear step

Reuse #1550's all-future equivalence, prior affine switched quotient, and the exact residual necessity criterion. Let full state domain V=R^n, a fixed model revision, admissible exogenous input set U, deterministic step T_u:V->V, declared observation O_u (can include discrete route/order/chart and legality). No continuity or derivatives assumed.

For any subspace N define
  D_N = {v : for all h in V, t in R, u in U,
                  T_u(h+t v)-T_u(h) in N}.
And N0 = {v : for all h,t,u, O_u(h+t v)=O_u(h)}.
These sets are linear subspaces: scalar closure is by replacing t; addition by telescoping through h+t v (full-domain translation is essential). D is monotone in N. Iterate N_{k+1}=N0 intersect D_{N_k}. This is descending and stabilizes after at most n strict dimension decreases; equality of adjacent subspaces is a fixed point. Its limit N* is the largest subspace with O constant along cosets and successor differences remaining in N*. Necessity: any stable linear codec kernel N is contained in N0, and N subset D_N, so inductively N subset every N_k. Sufficiency: h'-h in N* directly gives equal O and next coset; #1550 inputIndexedTraceDescent then gives all future observations. A basis B of annihilator N* gives reduced state Bh; choose a right inverse S and define reduced Tbar_u(z)=B T_u(Sz), Obar_u(z)=O_u(Sz). The choice of representative is immaterial by stability. This is a constructive mathematical classification of maximal stable *linear* full-domain codec for arbitrary nonlinear, possibly discontinuous deterministic transitions. It does not assert an efficient oracle for computing D_N or establish the fitted N*. On restricted reachable subsets the global translation closure need not hold; do not apply this as a minimality theorem there. Arbitrary nonlinear quotients use full trace equivalence instead.

In a differentiable full-domain cell with constant declared guard along the candidate directions, D_N reduces to Jacobian membership DT_u(h)v in N everywhere along those lines, by integrating the derivative; observer derivative similarly. ## 10. G6d — canonical-frame / stabilizer boundary gluing, not just rounding

Whole-source orbit descent F(s)=a^-1 P_K f(a.s) proves exact equivariance, not continuity. Even a continuous local f can cause a discontinuity. Let v be nonzero paired DC with both branches equal and all triad slots identical, so unsigned S3 fixes v and central negation sends it to -v; hold the identity conjugation control. Choose constant local f=v. At h=t v, t>0, canonical frame is positive, K=S3, F(h)=v. For t<0, F(h)=-v. At h=0, K=G, P_K v=0. Thus F jumps at zero. This is a valid abstract orbit-descent counterexample, NOT a claim that actual fitted source has this jump. It proves no uniform Lipschitz or rounding-only certificate can follow merely from equivariance. Canonical lexicographic frame can also jump at equal-prefix boundaries without stabilizer increase.

Exact local gluing: fix a finite partition of chart/guard strata indexed by mark a, stabilizer K, route/order, orientation and cap stratum. On each, source branch F_i is known. At h0, if every incident branch admits a continuous extension to h0, then F continuous there iff every extension equals F(h0). Necessity by incident sequences; sufficiency by finite union / sequential criterion. For stabilizer increase alone in a fixed limiting frame, incident K' subset K must satisfy P_K' f(c)=P_K f(c); if generic trivial stabilizer incident, requires f(c) in Fix(K). At different mark boundaries compare both decoded projected branch values, not just norms or local canonical values. For lexicographic order guard, every earlier coordinate equality must be exact or guaranteed by correlated intervals, then the first strict coordinate gap must dominate its difference error. A margin at the first nonzero difference alone is insufficient if earlier equalities can break. Stabilizer membership is exact equality; a nonmember can be certified by one strict difference, a member needs exact structural equality or exact decoded representation. Do not replace these by a tolerance and claim same chart.

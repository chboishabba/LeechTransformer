## G1. Exact signed ternary group algebra, full DC/phase operator family

R(x,y,z)=(y,z,x), J(x,y,z)=(z,y,x), R^3=J^2=I, JRJ=R^-1. Global sign -I commutes with both and is not J. Under C3 x global C2 the 27 coefficient triples have six orbits of sizes 1,2,6,6,6,6. Six-coefficient operator A=sum_(k=0..2)(a_k R^k+b_k R^k J). Each coefficient is ternary at fixed unit scale. Pure circulant has 3 independent scalars vs 9 entries of dense block; reflection extension has 6 stored coefficients but only 5 independent real DOF. Indeed sum R^k=sum R^k J=all-ones matrix, and flattening the six operators has rank 5 (exact). All preserve the DC line and phase plane. The vector space of all maps preserving both is R + Mat_2(R), dimension 5, so this is exactly the full DC/phase-preserving algebra. It does NOT contain general DC-to-phase coupling. Group-algebra membership is not equivariance to the full group: to commute with both R and J the phase action must be scalar; a generic reflection mixture fails to commute with R.

Gauge: adding t*(1,1,1,-1,-1,-1) to coefficients leaves A identical. This is the entire kernel. Flip signs of the last three coordinates, then equivalence is translation by t*(1,1,1,1,1,1). For ternary coefficients every class has one representative with minimum -1; shift down by min+1. Hence 3^6-2^6=665 distinct maps from 729 words. Exact enumeration ranks (distinct maps): rank0 1, rank1 76, rank2 156, rank3 432. This is not a 665-parameter model or storage below its alphabet lower bound. Expanded entries may be +/-2; no claim that a six-coefficient sum is a dense ternary matrix. Scale must be separately accounted for.

Pure circulants A=aI+bR+cR² satisfy d=a+b+c, q=a²+b²+c²-ab-bc-ca, det=dq. All 27 rank counts: 0:1,1:2,2:6,3:18. Same original affine tagged recurrence with other experts identity realizes 9,10,11,12 DOF via I,-R-R²,R,0 respectively; here the tagged-domain theorem is reused, not a new global dimension bound.

## G2. Generated symmetric-square interaction representation

Q(v)=(x²,y²,z²,xy,yz,zx), Phi(v)=(v,Q(v)). For arbitrary linear A define Sym²(A) uniquely by Q(Av)=Sym²(A)Q(v). This is the monomial (not orthonormal) basis. It has six coordinates. Multiplicativity follows from substitution, or spanning by squares via polarization; no invertibility required. Phi(Av)=(A direct-sum Sym²(A))Phi(v). For signed permutation generators Sym²(A) is a signed permutation of six coordinates. In particular Sym²(-A)=Sym²(A), hence original global sign acts as -I on V and +I on Sym²V; Q alone loses sign. The graph has only three independent real DOF, not nine. On the ambient linear nine-space rank is r+r(r+1)/2 when rank A=r: prove via invertible left/right rank reduction to diag(I_r,0), whose square has r(r+1)/2 surviving monomials. Thus ambient ranks 0,2,5,9 occur 1,2,6,18 times for ternary circulants. On the graph image only r continuous dimensions remain. This is separate from recurrent state dimension.

Monster dual analogy: on a marked cubic line chi, Sym²(chi)=chi^-1 while Sym²(chi^vee)=chi. For invertible group representation actions one can use contragredient A^-T; on orthogonal permutations it coincides with A in real orthonormal coordinates, but marked complex eigenlines pair with inverse characters. Do not use inverse-transpose on singular learned operators. The source single-triad forward lift uses Sym²(A), not independent dual data. General learned sums are not representations of S3 elements. Sym² is multiplicative, NOT additive: Sym²(I+R) != Sym²(I)+Sym²(R). For A=I+R+R², generated coefficients include 2, so independent ternary rounding of the lifted coefficients generally breaks exact transport. Generate after base quantization, not quantize both independently.

## G3. Real original directed pair observer: different 3+6=9 split

For independent triads x,h, the full outer product x h^T decomposes uniquely into symmetric 6 and skew 3; swap x,h negates the skew part, not the symmetric. Let U=(xh^T+hx^T)/2, K=(xh^T-hx^T)/2. The original self S=tr(U); forward F plus reverse B=2(U_01+U_12+U_20); F-B=(1,1,1) dot (x cross h). Thus the original ordered directed channels are exactly contractions of this pair lift, not replaced by the single-vector square. Polarization Q(x+h)-Q(x)-Q(h) yields the symmetric pair products. x cross h transforms under any orthogonal signed permutation g by det(g)g; the sum is cyclic-invariant and reflection-odd. Global simultaneous negation of x,h leaves BOTH pair parts invariant, unlike V plus Sym²V. Swapping roles preserves self/sum but reverses F-B. This proves why a symmetric-only representation erases ordered directional information. For full invertible representation actions the mixed vector-covector pairing transforms by x->Ax,h->A^-T h, preserving x^T h; this is not generally the same as acting on two original vectors by A. Preserve original role typing and source features.

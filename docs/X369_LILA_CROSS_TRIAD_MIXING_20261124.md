## G4a exact cross-triad expressivity

n input, m output triads. y_i=sum_j A_ij x_j. Original R(x,y,z)=(y,z,x), J(x,y,z)=(z,y,x). Let u=(1,1,1), p=(1,-1,0), q=(0,1,-1), D=span u, P=u^perp. C3 span I,R,R² is exactly commuting real DC scalar plus complex scalar on P (dimension 3). S3 basis I,R,R²,J,RJ is linearly independent, spans all DC/phase-preserving maps, dimension 5; omit R²J using the earlier gauge. Add up^T,uq^T,pu^T,qu^T, spanning Hom(P,D) and Hom(D,P) independently because p,q form basis of P and their dual functionals independent. Direct sum gives all M3(R). Across triad pairs dimensions 3mn,5mn,9mn. For square n=m, simultaneous Fourier chart gives C3 M_n(R) ⊕ M_n(C), S3 M_n(R) ⊕ M_{2n}(R); full M_{3n}(R). With unequal m,n use rectangular Hom spaces, not algebras. Generic S3 maps do not commute with R. Full real coefficients realize any dense matrix, not necessarily same finite ternary alphabet. Five ternary coordinates are a gauge-fixed *subset* of six-coefficient ternary image (243 vs 665); deleting a redundant coefficient can drive others outside [-1,1]. No claim of preserving all 665 at five trits. Full 9 ternary coordinates are a different discrete codebook than dense 9 ternary entries; each independent basis makes map injective. Expanded weights can exceed ±1. Bias must also be constrained for any full-layer equivariance claim; arbitrary source bias preserved in adapter.

## G4b generated local vs joint quadratic interactions

For v=(x_1,...,x_n), Q_all(v)=v v^T in symmetric coordinates, splits into n local Sym²(R³) blocks (6n) and n(n-1)/2 cross blocks x_i x_j^T (9 each): total 6n+9n(n-1)/2=(3n)(3n+1)/2. Under general cross-triad A, (A v)(A v)^T=A v v^T A^T, so exact full lifted representation is generated, not independent weights. In particular local Q(sum A_ij x_j) includes mixed terms from distinct j. Local even packets alone do not linearly close: x=(e1,e1), x'=(e1,-e1) have same per-triad squares, but after y=x1+x2 their square is 4 e1e1^T vs zero. Even retaining linear x alongside local Q does not yield a *linear* map to missing cross products on unrestricted real domain (different polynomial multidegrees). Nonlinear reconstruction is possible because original x retained: execute base mix then generate local Q, no materialized global quadratic tensor required for those requested outputs. Ambient lift dimension does not equal graph DOF. Signed cross products preserve relative sign and ordered role residual; do not identify symmetric square with original ordered pair.

## G4c exact original directed consumer transport

Original T_k(x,h) per triad=x_i^T R^k h_i (k=0,1,2), residual coherent R4 first rotates activation. For independent cross-triad maps A,B with m output triads and per-output original directed head coefficients w_ik, define W=diag_i sum_k w_ik R^k. Then source directed score on mixed vectors equals x^T A^T W B h = sum_(j,l) x_j^T (sum_i A_ij^T W_i B_il) h_l. This is exact on original bilinear consumer for any real coefficients; include original phase rotation in A when applicable. If A,B C3-block family, all pullback blocks remain circulant (transpose and multiplication closed); if S3, preserve DC/phase, but need all five channels, not only three original directed aggregates. If full, may need all nine. Concrete witness S3 reflection J is not circulant: its bilinear functional cannot factor linearly through only S,F,B on all x,h (basis vectors prove equality would force J circulant). Cross blocks j!=l cannot be recovered from only same-index pair aggregates, by choosing x only in j and h only in l. No general equivalence to original entire router: unary magnitude, absolute C2 baseline, orientation, FFN, transition, TopK and inactive histories remain original owners. This is a mathematical pullback, not a claim of executed full source router parity.

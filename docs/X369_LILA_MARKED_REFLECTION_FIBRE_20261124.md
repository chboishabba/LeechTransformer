## G5b1. Two signed charts and their typed residual

Reuse G4e sigma: C3-invariant, antipodally odd, zero iff zero, defined via lex maximum of the simultaneous cyclic orbit. Define Omega(v)=(sigma(v),sigma(Jv)). JR=R^-1 J implies R invariance, J swaps components, -I negates both. eta=(Omega_0+Omega_1)/2, xi=(Omega_0-Omega_1)/2. Both ternary; eta*xi=0; eta²+xi²=1 off zero and 0 at zero. J fixes eta and flips xi; global negation flips both. Thus retain a reflection-even signed coordinate and a reflection-odd signed residual, neither is the spatial Fourier DC/phase coordinate. At Jv=-v, eta=0, xi=±1; at Jv=v, xi=0, eta=±1 off zero. Converse not claimed; this two-sign chart is not a complete orbit invariant or injective codec. In particular it is cyclic invariant and cannot alone recover phase.

## G5b2. Reversible complete marked fibre

On any stack of ordered triads simultaneous action is faithful as a representation, not necessarily free on an individual point. Define c(v)=lex max_{g in G} gv and K_c={g:gc=c}. All marks for v are one left coset gK_c, i.e. right multiplication by stabilizer is a gauge. Codec E(v)=(c(v),gK_c), decode D(c,gK)=gc. Well-defined because changing representative g->gk leaves gc unchanged; E,D mutual inverses between raw states and valid canonical marked states. Transport by h is (c,hgK), exactly satisfies composition; choose min group tuple of each coset for deterministic storage. Zero K=G so one mark, nonzero constant triads K contains S3; all other cases classified by actual stabilizer. This is reversible rechart, not behavioural quotient or claim of compression. Fibre cardinality |G|/|K| is the exact number of distinguishable original states over c; any lossless code given c requires at least that many marks. Cannot discard sign even if antipode lies in same S3 orbit.

Hence M=(u+v)/2, D=(u-v)/2 satisfy M(Jz)=J M(z), D(Jz)=-J D(z), both rotate and are antipodally odd. Reverse weld u=M+D, v=M-D. Zero z yields both 0 for finite f(0).
